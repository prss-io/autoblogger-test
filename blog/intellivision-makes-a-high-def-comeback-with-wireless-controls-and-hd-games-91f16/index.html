Intellivision Makes a High-Def comeback with Wireless Controls and HD Games

19 Oct, 2025
Photo by Mao Batista via Pexels
Gaming enthusiasts and retro game fans are buzzing over Atari's latest project, a collaboration with Plaion to bring back the Intellivision console. If you're familiar with gaming history, the name Intellivision may evoke memories of its heyday in the late '70s and early '80s when it was one of Atari’s toughest competitors. The new release isn't just a nostalgic trip down memory lane; it's an innovative blend of old charm and modern conveniences.

The newly announced Intellivision Sprint aims to capture both the aesthetic appeal of its predecessor and the convenience of today’s gaming standards. Sporting a sleek design reminiscent of the original console with gold and black accents, the new Sprint model doesn’t skimp on nostalgia but comes packed with up-to-date features such as wireless controllers, HDMI connectivity, and a collection of 45 built-in games.

Wireless Control Freedom

The most striking feature of the Intellivision Sprint is its wireless controller setup. For those who remember the wired controllers with their unique dials and number pads (a stark contrast to Atari's joystick), this update feels like a breath of fresh air. These controllers can now be used without the hassle of wires, connecting seamlessly via Bluetooth to the console. What’s even better? They come equipped with charging docks that power them up when you plug them back into the system.

HD Resolution and Built-In Games

The Sprint isn’t just about wireless controls; it's also designed for modern display standards. Connecting directly to your TV via a single HDMI cable, this retro console offers high-definition resolution without any complicated setup processes. A collection of 45 classic games is built into the console, ensuring you have plenty to play right out of the box. Some of these include beloved titles like Boulder Dash and Astrosmash, alongside sports games that were instrumental in making the original Intellivision a household name.

Expansion Capabilities

While it doesn’t support cartridge loading (a feature sorely missed by some retro game purists), the Intellivision Sprint does offer USB-A ports to expand its capabilities. With these ports, players can plug in additional titles from a digital library or use adapters for original Intellivision controllers to maintain that authentic feel. This dual nature of accessibility and nostalgia keeps gamers satisfied on both fronts.

Preorder and Shipping Details

The excitement around this release is palpable among gaming communities, with preorders already open at an affordable price point of $149.99. Fans eager to relive the past while enjoying contemporary gaming features can secure their own Intellivision Sprint now, with deliveries expected as early as December 5th, 2025.

This comeback of a classic competitor serves not only as a nod to gaming history but also as an innovative step forward. With its blend of retro charm and modern technology, the Intellivision Sprint promises to be more than just another nostalgic product—it's an exciting new entry in the world of gaming-news that caters equally to enthusiasts craving old favorites and newcomers looking for a slice of gaming heritage with contemporary convenience.
